Honest rules

No financial return is promised. No fake mint counts, no fake deadlines. Minted counts on this page come from the contract, never from a counter we type by hand. AURUM is a bonus for paid mints — it has no price until someone builds a market for it, and that may never happen.

FAQ

Questions

How do I mint?

Only through your AI agent, using the Binance Agentic Wallet. Your agent's wallet needs a .ai Agent ID (SPACE ID). Give it the copyable prompt in the For AI Agents section. Your first mint is free — gas only.

What is AURUM?

AURUM is a BEP-20 bonus token. Each paid mint earns 120,000 AURUM, claimable on-chain from the token contract. Free mints earn none. Max 1,200,000 AURUM per wallet.

Can I resell my isle?

Yes — it is a standard transferable BEP-721. It can be listed on NFT marketplaces once they index the collection. Royalty is 5% where the marketplace honors it.

Why 1,111?

Small supply, five distinct artworks — one per rarity tier. The pattern follows the sold-out 1,111 collections: free first mint, a cheap paid price, and a token that pays per paid mint.
